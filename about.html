Executive biography

A growth executive who leads from the inside.

Moshe Farber is a Fractional CMO and Executive Growth Partner to business owners and leadership teams. For more than 15 years he has led marketing, strategy and customer experience: first as an agency founder, then as the full-time CMO of two fast-growing companies, and since 2023 as the founder of C-Level Accelerator.

As Chief Marketing Officer of a commercial building maintenance company, he built the marketing function, led a full rebrand and gave each service line its own focus. Over roughly six years the company grew into the largest in its field in the Eastern United States.

He went on to serve as CMO of a New York mortgage company through a national expansion, with new offices in New Jersey, Maryland, Texas, California, Florida and other states. He ran broker recruitment and borrower growth as two parallel strategies, across the Jewish community and the general market. His record includes 7 years in a row on the Inc. 5000 list of America’s fastest-growing companies, across companies he has led marketing for.

In 2023 he founded C-Level Accelerator Corp. to bring that executive leadership to growing companies on a fractional basis: part-time in hours, full-time in responsibility. Both companies he served as full-time CMO later brought him back as their fractional CMO. His work spans strategic planning, marketing and brand strategy, customer experience and leadership development, with one aim: marketing, sales and service working as one system, so growth holds.

Alongside his fractional work, Moshe offers business coaching and consulting with Rock Leadership Solutions (RLS), where he leads coaching groups and advises owners one-on-one. He also leads full-day leadership-team workshops for clarity and goal planning, as a single session or on a set rhythm through the year.

He is the author of the forthcoming book The Marketing Leader You Didn’t Know You Needed: Inside the Role of a Fractional CMO That Turns Strategy Into Growth, and writes The Marketing Mindset: Daily Sparks for Growth, a daily note for business owners. He studied business at Touro College.

How I Think About Growth

I’ve always enjoyed understanding how businesses work. Not just the marketing, but the people, the decisions, the challenges, and the opportunities that aren’t always obvious at first.

When I walk into a business, I don’t start by asking what we should advertise next. I want to understand where the business is today, where the owner wants to take it, and what’s standing in the way.

Sometimes the issue is marketing. Sometimes it’s the sales process, customer experience, internal communication, or simply a lack of clear direction. Often, it’s a combination of things that nobody has fully connected.

That’s the part I enjoy most. Asking questions, challenging assumptions, connecting the dots, and helping people see their business from a different perspective.

I also believe that good ideas are only valuable when something happens with them. I like being involved, working with people, bringing clarity to decisions, and making sure the right things actually move forward.

My approach isn’t about coming in with all the answers. It’s about understanding the business, working alongside its leadership, and finding the right path forward together.

Because meaningful growth doesn’t always come from doing more. Sometimes it starts by seeing things differently.
