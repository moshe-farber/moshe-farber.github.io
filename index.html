When I get involved

When the parts stop working together.

Most businesses don’t struggle because of one department. They struggle when leadership, strategy, marketing, sales, customer experience and execution stop working together.

  • Growth has stalled and no one can quite explain why.
  • Marketing keeps changing, but results don’t.
  • Teams are busy and every priority feels urgent, but no one is moving in the same direction.
  • The founder is the business’s biggest bottleneck.
  • Customer experience feels different depending on who you ask.
  • The business is entering a new, more complex stage, or launching a new venture or product.

Real Businesses. Real Challenges. Meaningful Progress.

Every business has its own challenges. Here are a few spotlights from my work alongside owners and leadership teams across different industries, much of it ongoing.

A growing professional services firm

Spotlight

A strong professional services firm had grown faster than its brand, client experience and outreach. Together we strengthened its positioning, client experience and communications, and built industry-specific marketing and business development initiatives around the people it serves best.

A large pharmaceutical company, consumer & wholesale

Spotlight

After years of switching agencies, the owner felt nobody understood the company. Discovery with the owner, head of sales and head of operations showed three different pictures. One workshop aligned them on who they are, and surfaced a new opportunity we took to market as a new brand.

A commercial building maintenance company

Spotlight

A fast-growing company needed a real marketing function and a brand that could keep up. We built the marketing function, led a full rebrand and gave each service line its own focus as it grew into the largest in its field in the Eastern U.S.

A New York mortgage lender

Spotlight

The company was ready to go national, but had to grow brokers and borrowers at the same time. I led marketing through an expansion across several states, building the broker network and the borrower base in parallel.

A software development company

Spotlight

Months of advertising hadn’t brought a single lead. The ads spoke to a very narrow audience in a place that needed a broader message. We also raised pricing and split the services into two focused teams: custom software and off-the-shelf implementation.

A consumer internet technology launch

Spotlight

A solo visionary saw what frustrated users about every product in his category. Working with him and a senior software architect, we defined the pain points, positioning and brand, built the go-to-market and the right partnerships, grew the team, and guided the product through alpha, beta and early access toward general availability.

A payroll & workforce technology company

Spotlight

The company had grown into far more than payroll, but the market still saw a traditional payroll provider. We moved its positioning toward a broader workforce management platform, and connected marketing with sales leadership, internal accountability and what customers actually needed.

An AI software company

Spotlight

The founder had built his own AI model and needed it to stand out. We shaped a distinctive offer, found the niche market it fits best, and built a path to steady, recurring revenue without constant effort behind it.

An established e-commerce & equipment business

Spotlight

Marketing, e-commerce, leadership, creative and operations were each pushing their own priorities. We brought those teams into alignment, improved communication and accountability, and tied business priorities directly to day-to-day execution.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Straight answers on how a fractional CMO engagement works.

Ask your own question →
What is a fractional CMO, and how is it different from a full-time CMO?

A fractional CMO is a senior marketing leader who provides strategic vision, leadership and execution support on a part-time, flexible basis. You get executive-level marketing leadership for the time and expertise your business needs, without the commitment of a full-time executive hire.

How are you different from a marketing agency?

Agencies are strong at running campaigns and producing work. I sit on your leadership team: setting the direction, aligning marketing with business goals, and leading execution, often together with your agencies, so everything delivers results.

What does your process look like, and do you do the work yourself?

It starts with a deep-dive discovery and a clear roadmap. Then we align the team, execute, and refine over time, at a pace built around your business. I personally lead all strategy and oversight, and roll up my sleeves on execution, either directly or by guiding your team and trusted specialists, so everything aligns and delivers.

Can you work with our existing team, or help us build one?

Both. I work with your current staff, freelancers and agencies to get everyone aligned on the same goals, with no need to replace anyone unless the business needs different resources. If you don’t have a marketing team yet, I help you build the right team and bring in reliable partners.

What if we already have a CMO or head of marketing?

I can work alongside existing leadership, offering an outside perspective, sharper strategy, or interim guidance while the team is in transition.

How quickly will we see results?

Clarity and direction start right away; after the first strategy session, most leaders already feel more focused. Business results depend on your goals and the complexity of the work, and most clients see meaningful progress within a few months.

How do customer experience and culture fit into marketing?

Marketing isn’t only leads and campaigns. It’s a consistent experience that attracts and keeps customers. If the culture inside is disconnected, or customers feel let down after the sale, no amount of advertising fixes that. Marketing sets the expectations, customer experience fulfills them, and culture sustains them. When those three are aligned, growth becomes easier and lasts.
